BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

A few
good questions.

Get your bearings.

What does AntTrail help me do?

AntTrail helps you define your ideal customer, discover companies, and research why they may fit. Specialist AI agents bring findings, sources, and missing information into a shortlist you can review before choosing the next step.

Do I need an existing lead list?

No. Start with an audience description or an existing ideal customer profile. The ICP strategist can also propose targeting criteria for your review. Discovery does not require a saved account database.

Where does the research come from?

Research can use supported AI public-web search and authorized data-provider APIs. Direct signal connections are separate from AI research. Some operations require your own provider account, API access, or a vendor contract. Provider charges may apply.

How much control do I have?

You review the plan and set limits before execution. Approved hosted runs can continue after you close the browser, with saved history, checkpoints, and cancellation. Planning can itself incur model usage. AI findings may be incomplete or incorrect, so review important facts before acting.

Will agents automatically contact people?

No. Discovery produces research for your review. Contact verification, CRM transfer, and approved sending are separate steps with their own setup. A business signal does not establish buying intent, and email verification does not establish permission to contact someone.

How can I get into the app?

Workspace access currently requires an invitation. If you've been invited, accept your invitation and use Open app to sign in. You can explore the prepared example on this page without an account.
